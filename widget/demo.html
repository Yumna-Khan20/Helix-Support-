Core Capabilities

Engineered for High-Volume Support

Built with reliable free-tier services to give you enterprise customer support at zero infrastructure cost.

🤖

Google Gemini AI Powered

Uses Gemini 1.5 Flash with custom system instructions to answer accurately, warm, and concisely strictly from your knowledge base.

⚡

Smart Escalation Engine

Monitors trigger keywords (refund, cancel, legal, broken, human), repeated question loops, and customer frustration sentiment.

🗄️

Supabase Postgres Database

Automatically records tickets with complete chat transcripts, session IDs, reasons, and resolution states in Supabase Postgres.

📢

Instant Discord Alerts

Pushes rich Discord embeds directly into your team's support channel with the last 5 transcript turns and escalation reason.

🛡️

Support Admin Dashboard

Password-gated web dashboard at /admin.html to search, inspect complete conversation transcripts, and resolve tickets.

📦

Zero-Build Drop-In Widget

Self-contained JavaScript widget (widget.js) that injects scoped HTML/CSS, supports mobile fullscreen, and saves session state.

Drop-in Website Integration

Copy and paste this snippet right before the closing </body> tag on any website:

<!-- Helix Support Widget -->
<script 
  src="http://localhost:3000/widget.js" 
  data-company="Helix Support" 
  data-endpoint="http://localhost:3000/api/chat">
</script>

✨ When deployed to Render.com or your production server, simply replace http://localhost:3000 with your production URL.
